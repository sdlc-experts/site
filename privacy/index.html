Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who we are

SDLC Experts is based in Israel and is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: hello@sdlc-experts.com.

What we collect and why

  • Demo bookings. When you book a demo, our scheduling provider, Cal.com, collects your name, email address and anything you write in the booking form. We use it to hold the meeting and follow up about it. Legal basis: steps you ask us to take before a possible contract.
  • Emails and messages. If you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we keep the conversation to reply and to keep a record of our business contacts. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you.
  • Website hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security and to operate the service.

When you click to book a demo, the site opens Cal.com's booking calendar in a pop-up, so Cal.com receives your IP address and the details you enter.

Ad measurement. We advertise on Google. For visitors outside Europe, the site loads the Google Ads tag, which uses cookies to tell us whether a visit from one of our ads led to a demo booking. Google receives your IP address, the pages you visit on this site and an ad click identifier. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing which ads work. The tag does not load for visitors in Europe, and it does not load if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Apart from that, the site runs no analytics.

Who we share it with

Only the services we use to run the business: Cal.com (scheduling), Zoho (email), GitHub (hosting), Google (advertising and ad measurement) and, if you contact us there, WhatsApp. Some of them process data outside the European Economic Area under their own safeguards for international transfers. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and contact details for as long as needed to follow up and run our business relationship, and delete them on request unless we must keep them for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. Write to hello@sdlc-experts.com. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
